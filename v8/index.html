  1. DefineYou + the Mind

    Start from what you already know.

    You share your brief, the outcome you’re accountable for, and any past research or data. Noetic Mind keeps the outside world in view too, refreshing web, social and market signals every day.

  2. ProgressThe human part

    Hear why customers really switch.

    Twenty-minute Jobs-to-be-Done interviews about real decisions: what people were trying to get done, what pushed them and what held them back. The Noetic Copilot guides the interviewer live. Twelve to fifteen stories make a round.

  3. OutcomeThe Mind drafts · leadership agrees

    Agree the number that matters.

    The Mind proposes the one measurable outcome the product should move, with its baseline. Leadership ratifies it, so the team argues about evidence, not about what success means.

  4. OpportunityAutomated · you approve

    Find the struggle worth solving.

    Interviews are clustered into jobs, one cluster for each job, and every struggling moment is mapped as an opportunity with its evidence state. The Mind recommends one to pursue. You approve it.

  5. Causal learningAutomated · reviewed with you

    Understand what has to be true.

    Before anyone designs a solution, the Mind maps the chain that would produce progress, from inputs to outcome, and separates what you control from the noise.

  6. Delivery & impactAutomated · your team ships

    Test before you build.

    Three materially different options, the riskiest assumption behind them, and a test card with its pass mark set before the results arrive. After you ship, impact is measured and fed back into the system.

  7. The treeUpdated continuously · you approve

    Every answer updates the map.

    Everything lands in one living Progress-to-Outcome Tree, from outcome to opportunity to test. The Mind suggests each change and nothing updates until a person approves it. The next best action is always on screen.

Who does what

The Mind does the heavy lifting. People make the calls.

Noetic Mind

  • Keeps your context current, every day
  • Turns interviews into jobs, forces and timelines
  • Drafts the outcome, the opportunities and the causal map
  • Designs the tests and reads the impact
  • Writes the living report and keeps the decision log

Your team

  • Shares the brief and access to customers
  • Ratifies the outcome with leadership
  • Approves, edits or rejects every suggested change
  • Runs the tests and ships
  • Makes the call

Noetic

  • Installs and configures the system
  • Sets up the interview Copilot
  • Trains your team to work with the Mind
  • Coaches the difficult decisions
