The same year, run again

This time the team asks first

Same team, same idea, same budget. Before any code is written they spend six weeks on one cycle of Noetic Mind: five steps, each feeding a shared picture of the customer.

Day one · The Mind

They write down what they believe

Each person says what they think customers want from the feature. Noetic Mind records every belief as an assumption, waiting to be tested.

Weeks 1 and 2 · Listen

They ask customers what they actually did

A dozen customers describe the last time they wrestled with the job the feature is meant to help. Nobody is asked what they would like.

Week 3 · Aim

They agree the one number that matters

In this example it is renewals: where the figure stands today and how far it has to move. Every idea from here on is judged against it.

Week 3 · Choose

They pick the struggle most worth solving

The interviews turn up several struggles, and one stands out. For exampleCustomers do not need help writing their reports. They need to trust the numbers before they send one.

Week 4 · Work back

They trace the route back from the number

Starting at renewals, they work backwards one step at a time until they reach something the team can change this month.

Weeks 5 and 6 · Test

They test small, then build

Three small versions go in front of customers. One of them moves the number, and that is the one the budget goes on.

Month 16 · The question

This time there is an answer

The finance director asks why it was built. The answer is on record: who was asked, what they said and what the test showed. Then the cycle starts again, and the team knows a little more each time.
