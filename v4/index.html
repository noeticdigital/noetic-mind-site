A few practical questions

We already talk to customers. What changes?

The connection between those conversations and product decisions. The system makes clear which outcome you’re pursuing, which opportunity matters, what evidence supports it, and what assumption to test next.

Is this software or consulting?

It’s a managed operating system installed inside your team, with training and ongoing coaching. You’re not buying a standalone software subscription, and you’re not outsourcing discovery.

Who takes part, and how often?

A small cross-functional team — typically product, design, and engineering — runs one customer conversation and one decision review each week. Leadership joins once a quarter to agree the outcomes the team is working toward.
