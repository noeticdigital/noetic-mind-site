The stages

Interviews are the first layer. Decisions come from the rest.

Most teams stop once the customer research is written up. It’s useful, then it dates. Noetic Mind takes the work through every stage, and what you learn from testing goes straight back into the next round of interviews.

  1. DefineWrite down what you already believe and what you need to learn.
  2. Jobs to be DoneLearn why customers really hire your product.
  3. OutcomeAgree one customer behaviour to change.
  4. OpportunityRank the problems worth solving.
  5. Causal modelWork out why a change should work.
  6. DeliveryTest with customers before you build.
