Let’s run that same year one more time↓

Start by discovering what is driving customers’ decisions

Before anyone writes code, you find out what matters to customers. Noetic Mind guides you through each step, in about six weeks. Same team. Same idea. Same budget.

Write down what you think

Everyone says what they believe customers need. Noetic Mind records each belief as an assumption to test. In this exampleThe assumption: customers need a faster way to write reports.

Sit down with customers

You’re listening for why people buy: what they need done, how they want to feel, and how they want to be seen. Functional, emotional and social. So you ask what they did, and what held them back. In this exampleWhat customers tell you: writing the report is easy. Checking the numbers takes hours, and nobody wants to send the board a wrong figure.

Pick one number

Choose the number the business most needs to move. Every idea is judged against that number: where it stands today, and where it needs to be. In this exampleThe number: revenue.

Choose the problem worth solving

Customers describe several struggles. You choose the one that matters most to them and to your number. In this exampleThe problem you choose: customers can’t trust the numbers before they send a report. Not the one you started with.

Work back to something you can change

Start at the number and ask what would have to happen to move that number. Keep asking until you reach something you can change now. In this exampleWhat you can change now: show customers where each figure came from.

Test before you commit

A few small versions go in front of customers. You see which one helps. Then you decide what deserves to be built. In this exampleWhat you test: three ways of showing where a figure came from, each tried on real reports.

This time, you have an answer

Someone asks why you built it. You show them what you assumed, what customers showed you, and what happened when you tried a fix. Everything you learn stays with your team, so the next decision starts from a better place.

Noetic Mind vs. your current AI

AI knows what people have already said. It doesn’t know why your customers buy. Nobody has written that down yet.

  • Someone has to ask

    The real reasons come out when you sit down with customers and listen. Noetic trains your people to do that.

  • A summary isn’t a decision

    AI can summarise a hundred interviews. Noetic Mind tells you what they mean for what you build next. Then you test it.

  • People make the call

    Noetic Mind suggests. Your team decides. Noetic has been helping teams make these calls for over a decade.

Why continuous discovery?

A one-off project answers one question. Your team faces a new one every quarter and the market is always evolving.

  • Customers keep changing

    What mattered to customers last year may not matter now. A report starts going out of date the day it is delivered. Noetic Mind keeps listening, so you see the change as it happens.

  • There is always a next decision

    After this feature comes the next one. Each deserves the same evidence before you commit people, time and money.

  • Every cycle makes the next one more valuable

    Each cycle builds on everything the last one learnt, so every decision rests on more evidence than the one before.
