The system

From conversations to a decision.

  1. Listen

    Every week, your team hears from customers about the progress they’re trying to make. Each conversation is a signal.

  2. Understand

    Signals are compared and connected until the patterns are clear enough to act on.

  3. Decide

    Patterns resolve into one decision your team can defend, and the smallest test that would prove it wrong.
