A story you may recognise

The feature was built well. Nobody paid for it.

It starts in a planning meeting. Every competitor is announcing an AI feature, the board would like one too, and the team has a good idea. The engineers are strong. The budget is there.

A year later it ships, on time and working as designed. Customers try it, say it is impressive, and carry on as before. Renewals do not move.

Then the finance director asks why it was built. The honest answer is that it seemed right in the room.

Nobody was careless. The team simply had no way of knowing, before they started, what customers would pay for.

A composite of a pattern common across software companies. It describes no particular client.

The same year, run differently

The money goes on what customers will pay for, and the reasoning is written down for the board and the finance director.

How it works

One cycle, run again and again

Noetic Mind is software, training and support, set up inside your team. At its centre is the Mind, which holds what you know about your market and your customers. Five steps feed it, and each cycle leaves you surer of what to build next.

At the centre · The Mind

It starts with what your team already believes

The Mind reads what is said about your market and asks each person on the team for their view. Every belief is treated as an assumption until customers confirm it.

Step 1 · Listen

Hear what customers actually did

Your people interview customers about real decisions: the last time they switched, struggled or gave up. We train them to do it, and our Copilot helps during each call.

Step 2 · Aim

Agree the one number that matters

The team picks a customer behaviour to change, where it stands today and how far it has to move. Everything after this is judged against it.

Step 3 · Choose

Pick the struggle most worth solving

The struggles customers described are laid out in the order they meet them. You choose the one that matters most to them and to your number. The rest wait their turn.

Step 4 · Work back

Trace the route back to what you can change

From the goal, the route is worked backwards until it reaches something the team can change this month. Then it is checked forwards.

Step 5 · Test

Test small, then build

Several small versions are tried with customers. The one that moves the number is built, and what you learn starts the next cycle.

After every cycle

You know what to build next, and you can show why

  • What to build, what to test first and what to drop
  • The evidence behind each call, ready for the board
  • A team that gets better at this with every cycle
